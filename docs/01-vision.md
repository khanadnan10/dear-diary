# 01 — Product Vision

## Purpose

Dear Diary is a minimalist, privacy-first journaling app built with Flutter. It exists as a quiet space for emotional unloading — not productivity tracking, not social sharing, not AI-powered reflection.

---

## Vision Statement

> A private emotional unloading space that feels safe, fast, and free of judgment.

---

## Goals

| Goal | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Emotional safety** | Users must feel the app is a secure vault — nothing leaves the device |
| **Zero friction capture** | Opening to writing/recording in under 2 seconds |
| **True privacy** | No accounts, no telemetry, no internet dependency |
| **Calm UX** | No notifications urging journaling, no streaks, no badges |
| **Longevity** | Entries must remain readable years later, regardless of app updates |

---

## Non-Goals (MVP)

These are explicitly excluded and must not creep into MVP scope:

- ❌ Cloud sync or backup
- ❌ AI-generated summaries or insights
- ❌ Mood tracking or tagging
- ❌ Streaks, gamification, or reminders
- ❌ Social sharing
- ❌ Complex analytics or dashboards
- ❌ Video journaling
- ❌ Markdown editor
- ❌ Community features

---

## Target User

Someone who:
- Journals privately and does not want a digital trail
- Values emotional release over structured documentation
- Prefers simplicity over power-user features
- Is security-conscious and distrusts cloud apps for personal writing

---

## Success Criteria (MVP)

- App opens and is ready to write in < 2 seconds after biometric unlock
- All entries are encrypted at rest; no plaintext ever hits disk
- Audio recordings are hidden from the device gallery
- App functions entirely offline
- No crash on any expected user interaction flow
